About.

Building language and multimodal AI that is accurate, explainable, and practical.

I am a Computer Science graduate from HITEC University, Taxila, Pakistan. My research focuses on multilingual NLP, large language models, and multimodal learning, with particular interest in low-resource languages such as Urdu and Roman Urdu.

I have worked on transformer-based classification, cross-lingual transfer, multimodal hate speech detection, and LLM-based text detoxification. I am also interested in retrieval-augmented generation for domain-specific question answering.
